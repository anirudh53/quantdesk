Quantdesk

Daily research evidence for your watchlist. Not buy/sell signals.

  • Attention ranks how unusual today's move and volume are. It says nothing about which way the price goes next.
  • "Move vs normal" = today's % move divided by the stock's typical daily move over the last 20 days (σ). Beyond ±2.5σ gets flagged.
  • Model probabilities only count if the model's own out-of-sample record supports them. "Weak/none" means ignore them.
  • Data is from Yahoo Finance via yfinance: delayed / end-of-day, can be wrong. Check primary sources before acting on anything.
